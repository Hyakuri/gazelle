## Stage 2 goal

Make the three-camera work recordings usable and trustworthy as input, synchronize the cameras, fix the known perception defects, and build the region-labelling tool that stage 3 will be evaluated against.

## Project goal (confirmed by the user on 2026-10-15)

Three fixed monocular cameras film one workstation: two on the desks facing the worker (front: カメラ2, right: カメラ1) and the overview camera 設備カメラ between the two desks, looking down at both. Camera positions are the same in every recording. The system will fuse the three views to estimate, over time, which work region the worker attends to: the regions the user drew on the overview camera (four target regions inside the work cell, drawn on 2026-10-17), other places in the cell, away from work, or absent. It will report time shares, dwell durations, transitions, and off-task time. Processing is offline. Multi-Pose integration is out of scope for now; multi-person inference and real-time processing are later goals.

## Findings that shaped this stage

- The current code cannot process the work data. All 11 AVI files contain empty packets, so OpenCV decodes one frame and the pipeline still reports success (GZ-21). 12 of 29 variable-frame-rate MP4 parts abort in MediaPipe on non-increasing timestamps (GZ-07, raised to P0).
- AVI container time drifts by about 0.2%, so the first plan synchronized the cameras by their burned-in clocks. The round-2 sync analysis then showed that the three clocks are set independently, up to about 3 s apart. Synchronization now uses image motion with the front camera as reference (D18, below).
- The face cameras see the face but not the desks; the overview camera sees the desks and hands but rarely the head. Region inference therefore needs multi-camera fusion (stage 3 proposal).
- `sample2.mp4` is an edited video from a different workplace, so validation moves to the work data.

Details: `docs/collab/analysis/2026-10-15-workdata-analysis.md`.

Sync analysis on 2026-10-17 (`docs/collab/evidence/2026-10-17-sync-analysis.md`):

- The three cameras' burned-in clocks are set independently. Against the front camera B, the right camera is off by −0.63 to +2.83 s, and the overview camera by −0.95 to +2.47 s with drift.
- The overview camera's displayed clock also steps by fractions of a second while its frame timing stays steady. The round 2 time maps split at those steps or drop them as outliers.
- Motion cross-correlation measures the offsets with a spread of 0.02–0.07 s, so the cameras can be aligned to within 0.2 s.
- D18, approved by the user on 2026-10-17: take B as the reference and calibrate the other two cameras from image motion (TASK-139). D13's time axis becomes "B's displayed clock, content-calibrated, 5 Hz". The old proxies and the 27-segment trial set serve tool trials only.

Round 3 review on 2026-10-17 (`docs/collab/evidence/2026-10-17-sync-validation-r3.md`):

- **The 18 new proxies are well synchronized.** Claude recomputed the residual lags independently with area-averaged thumbnails, masking the steps that span a source keyframe. In all six recordings, A against B and E against B meet the unchanged TASK-139 gates, with residual medians of 0.02–0.08 s.
- **The six residual failures in the first validation came from the measurement (GZ-29).** In three recordings the face cameras put a keyframe exactly every 2.0 s, which is 10 grid steps. Each keyframe adds a pulse to the frame-difference energy, so these pulses form a phase-locked comb, and linear downscaling from 1080p amplified it.
- **The overview camera of 20260911 has a step of about 0.46 s.** A revised step rule, tested on the real windows and on synthetic cases, calibrates it. TASK-140 also fixes the clock spot-check definition and then builds the formal dataset.
- **Other results:**
  - TASK-002: the frame-15 sample depends on the input encoding, not on the code (Q-011).
  - TASK-102: in single-person mode, a one-frame provisional head can delete a confirmed track's bridge (GZ-30).
  - The reference rays are distorted on non-square frames (GZ-28).

## 影响本阶段安排的发现

- 当前代码无法处理作业数据。11 个 AVI 文件都含空数据包，OpenCV 只解出 1 帧，管线仍报告成功（GZ-21）。29 个可变帧率 MP4 分段中有 12 个因时间戳不递增在 MediaPipe 中止（GZ-07，升为 P0）。
- AVI 容器时间漂移约 0.2%，最初计划按烧录的画面时钟同步。第 2 轮的同步分析发现，三台摄像机的画面时钟各自设定，最多相差约 3 秒。现在改为以前方摄像机为基准、用画面运动校准（D18，见下文）。
- 人脸摄像机看得到脸、看不到桌面；俯视摄像机看得到桌面与双手，却很少拍到头部。区域推断因此需要多机融合（第 3 阶段提案）。
- `sample2.mp4` 是另一工作场所的剪辑视频，验证改用作业数据。
